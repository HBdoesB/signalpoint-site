How it works

  1. We ask what your customers ask

    Twenty-five to thirty real buying questions for your category. The phrasing people actually type when they're deciding what to buy, not keywords from a planner.

  2. We record every brand named

    Who Google recommends, how often, and where you sit. Every answer is archived, so you read the original rather than our summary of it.

  3. We show what's different about them

    A measured comparison between you and the brands being recommended: product titles, marketplace presence, the sources Google cites, and the questions nobody has claimed yet.

This is not SEO, and your rankings will not tell you

In our own scan, one brand had its website cited as a source five separate times and was recommended by name zero times. Another was cited once and named in nineteen of twenty-three answers. Being the source Google reads is not the same as being the brand Google names — and your analytics cannot tell the two apart.

Meanwhile that answer sits above every organic result, and for a shopper choosing between four serums it often ends the search.

Nobody is measuring this in India

Global tools track AI visibility for US and European brands. None of them run Indian buying questions, in India, against Indian brands.

Questions people ask

Can I verify this myself?

Yes, and we would rather you did. Every report includes the exact questions we ran. Type one into Google, look at the AI answer at the top, and count. It takes about a minute, and it is the reason you do not have to take our word for anything.

Can you guarantee you will get my brand recommended?

No, and be suspicious of anyone who does. We have measured which things differ between recommended and invisible brands, and we can do that work. What nobody has established — including agencies charging a lakh a month — is a reliable cause. We will tell you what we measured, do the work, re-measure, and report the result honestly whichever way it goes.

Is this the same as SEO?

No. SEO decides which pages Google reads; this measures which brands Google names in the answer it writes. Our study found those are different lists — the most-cited site in the scan was never once recommended. Both are published in the data, so you can check it.
