What we do

Three steps. You can stop after the second one, and plenty of brands will.

  1. Measure

    We run 25–30 real buying questions for your category and record every brand the AI names. Your score, your competitors' scores, and the exact question each one won. Every answer archived so you read the original, not our summary of it.

  2. Plan

    A prioritised list of what to change, in order, each item tied to a specific page, listing or piece of content — not advice, instructions. Including the buying questions in your category where the AI currently names nobody. Those are the cheapest wins available to you and nobody is competing for them yet.

  3. Execute — optional

    Hand the plan to your team, or hand it to us. If it's us, we rewrite the product titles, write the content for the open questions, close the listing gaps, and then re-measure and tell you honestly whether it moved.

This is not SEO, and your rankings will not tell you

In our own scan, one brand had its website cited as a source five separate times and was recommended by name zero times. Another was cited once and named in nineteen of twenty-three answers. Being the source the AI reads is not the same as being the brand it names — and your analytics cannot tell the two apart.

Meanwhile that answer sits above every organic result, and for a shopper choosing between four serums it often ends the search.

Questions people ask

Can you guarantee you will get my brand recommended?

No, and be suspicious of anyone who does. We have measured which things differ between recommended and invisible brands — product title convention most clearly — and we can do that work. What nobody has established, including agencies charging a lakh a month, is a reliable cause. So we promise the work and the scoreboard, not the outcome: we measure, we change things, we re-measure, and you see whether it moved. If it didn't, we say so.

What do I actually receive for ₹15,000?

A document and a spreadsheet. The document is your score, who is being recommended instead, the open questions nobody has claimed, and a numbered list of what to change in what order — each item pointing at a specific page, listing or product title. The spreadsheet is that list as a tracker your team works through, plus every answer and source we captured as raw data. It is a plan, not a diagnosis.

Can I verify this myself?

Yes, and we would rather you did. Every report includes the exact questions we ran. Type one into Google, look at the AI answer at the top, and count. It takes about a minute, and it is the reason you do not have to take our word for anything.

Is this the same as SEO?

No. SEO decides which pages the AI reads; this measures which brands it names in the answer it writes. Our study found those are different lists — the most-cited site in the scan was never once recommended. Both are published in the data, so you can check it.

Which categories do you cover?

Our deepest data is in beauty, personal care and nutrition supplements, because that is where we have run the full study. The method works for any category where people ask buying questions — tell us yours and we will say honestly whether we can do it well.

Do you need access to my website or accounts?

No. Everything we measure is public. No logins, no analytics access, no personal data of any kind. For the Done For You tier we will need access to whatever we are editing, but not before you have bought it.
